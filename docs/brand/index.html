01 / Identity

A sealed boundary
around nothing.

The seal is the null set drawn as an enclosure: a closed square, cut on its diagonal into two interlocking halves, with an empty centre. Two halves for request and response; the cut is where every response is checked. Use the supplied artwork to preserve geometry, spacing and letterforms.

Construction

Square, cut, empty.

One half holds the top and left sides, the other the bottom and right. Each ends in a 45° cut; the gap between them sits on the null diagonal. The second half is shown lighter here only to show the split; the artwork uses one color.

  • Tile96 × 96 units
  • Enclosure12 to 84 on both axes; 12-unit stroke
  • CutParallel 45° edges on x + y = 86 and 106, about 14 units apart
  • Small master16-unit grid, whole-pixel 2-unit stroke, for 16–23 px
  • WordmarkIBM Plex Sans SemiBold, outlined, −1.5 tracking at 76 units
  • Lockup440 × 112
  • Use the complete lockup first.

    Introduce nullrpc with the symbol and wordmark together. The symbol alone works for avatars, favicons and established contexts.

  • Match the mark to its surface.

    Signal + Paper on dark. Deep signal + Carbon on light. Use the one-color files when color is unavailable.

  • Respect minimum sizes.

    Lockup: 144 px wide (38 mm in print). Wordmark: 112 px. Regular symbol: 24 px and up. Optical small symbol: 16–23 px.

Keep it intact

Do not stretch, rotate, round or outline the symbol. Do not close the gap, fill the centre, change the cut's angle or color the halves differently. No glow, gradient or drop shadow. Never place the logo over a busy image or a low-contrast background.

04 / Applications

One system.
Every surface.

The same geometry, palette and plain language across link previews, repository covers and developer interfaces. These examples show the system in use.

Composition

Align to a clear grid. Give the message and the mark their own space. Use hairlines and eyebrows to organize information.

Imagery

Prefer the symbol, diagrams, tables and code. Avoid coins, token motifs, padlocks and stock blockchain imagery.

Motion

Keep transitions brief and functional. Honor reduced-motion preferences. The identity works without animation.

05 / Voice

Speak like
infrastructure.

Precise, sober, operational. State what is available, what happened and what to do next. Use numbers and guarantees only where they are true.

Affordable RPC.

Write the brand as nullrpc, always lowercase. Keep the identity network-neutral; list live networks and archive capabilities in product details.

Affordability

“Affordable RPC.”

Talk about access and value. Publish exact rates and comparisons only when current pricing supports them.

Privacy

“Privacy in focus.”

Describe specific data practices when they are verified. Do not imply anonymity, zero logging or protections that are not documented.
